3. Brand application

Theme

Dark-first, as the guide decides. The site opens dark whatever the visitor's system setting. A Dark / Light switch in the footer changes the theme and is remembered in the visitor's browser. The hero band and the closing contact band stay Deep Navy in both themes.

  • All colours come from the section 7 tokens in the guide. Light theme uses the guide's light tokens unchanged.
  • Orange appears only on primary buttons, the unit intake point in the hero diagram, and form error text. Each screen-height view has one orange button at most; other actions are outline buttons or text links.
  • Green marks product and positive status only: the "What comes out" and "What you receive" bullets, the CO2 removed row in the report, the carbon flow in the diagram, fullerene pentagons, the "Available" chip and the "Why buy" rules.
  • Primary buttons carry navy text on orange (6.46:1). White on orange is not used.

Headings use sentence case. Chemical formulas use real subscripts (CO2, C60, SOx); "C60" in the company name is set plain. The logo typefaces (Ethnocentric, Venus Rising) are not loaded; the logo ships as outlined vector paths, so no font licence question arises on the website.

Logo

  • Inline SVG built from the guide's vector paths, coloured through theme tokens: light version on the light theme, dark version on the dark theme and in the Deep Navy bands.
  • Header and footer logo are 64 px high (about 143 px wide), above the guide's 140 px minimum. On phones 56 px high (125 px wide); this is below the minimum and should switch to the re-set lockup or the mark plus "GLOBAL" once those files exist.

Layout and shape

1200 px content width, 24 px gutters, breakpoints at 640 and 960 px. Section padding 64 px desktop, 40 px phone. Radius 6 px on buttons and inputs, 8 px on cards, none on full-width bands. No shadows; depth comes from borders and background steps. Motion is limited to 150 ms colour transitions and smooth anchor scrolling, both off when the visitor asks for reduced motion.

5. Components

Buttons

Primary: Orange 500 fill, navy text, hover Orange 400 (dark) or 600 (light). Secondary: 1.5 px outline in the brand colour, fills on hover. Text link: link colour, underline on hover. All at least 44 px high.

Status chips

Pill with a dot and a mono label. Green for confirmed ("Available for discussion now"). Steel for neutral or pending ("Specification to follow", "Under way"). The dot and wording carry the state, not colour alone.

Cards

Navy 800 on Navy 900 (dark) or Steel 50 on white (light), 1 px border, 8 px radius, 24 px padding. Bullets are short bars: blue for general lists, green for outcomes.

Callouts

Card with a 3 px left rule: blue for the Alberta TIER note, steel for "Under investigation" (MaaS).

Steps

Five columns on desktop, two on tablet, one on phone. Each has a 2 px top rule and a mono number 01 to 05; the numbering is real because the steps happen in order.

Report table

Styled as a report header plus a two-column table (field, what it tells you). Field names in mono. The CO2 removed row is green. Scrolls sideways inside its box on narrow phones. No example numbers.

Product cards

Illustration panel on the page background, then title, chip and text. The carbon black card is a wide feature card with the industries list in two columns.

Team cards

Name, mono role, one-paragraph background, email and the supplied LinkedIn icon (20 px image inside a 44 px tap target). No photos.

7. Interest form

Every "Register interest", "Host a unit", "Buy our carbon" and MaaS button opens the same modal form. The button pre-selects the interest (site, carbon, carbon reduction), and the visitor can change it.

  • States: errors show under each field and move focus to the first problem; the Send button reads "Sending…" while it works; success shows "Thank you. We have your details and will be in touch."; failure asks the visitor to retry or email rvisser@globalc60.com.
  • Privacy line under the button: "We use these details only to reply to you. We do not share them."
  • Spam: a hidden trap field (website) that people never fill in; submissions with it filled are dropped.

Connection

The form posts to Formspree at https://formspree.io/f/xyezaoww, which emails each submission to rvisser@globalc60.com with the subject "GlobalC60.com: new interest registration". The same URL is the form's action, so it still posts if JavaScript is off. The hidden spam field is Formspree's _gotcha.

After launch, send one test submission from globalc60.com and, in Formspree, restrict the form to the globalc60.com domain.

9. Accessibility

  • Colour pairings are those checked in the brand guide: AA or better in both themes.
  • Focus ring: 2 px, Navy 500 on light and Blue 300 on dark, offset 2 px, never removed.
  • Skip link to the main content; landmarks for header, main, footer; each section labelled by its heading.
  • Touch targets at least 44 × 44 px, including LinkedIn icons and the modal close button.
  • Form fields have visible labels and linked error messages; the modal returns focus to the button that opened it and closes with Escape.
  • Every drawing has a text description; the hero diagram describes the flow in order.
  • Reduced motion setting turns off smooth scrolling and transitions.

Where to keep building

This page was built in the Claude app, which is well suited to a single-file site with review by comments. Move to Claude Code when any of these happen: the site grows to the six pages in the brand guide, you want the files in a Git repository with automatic deploys to Netlify or Cloudflare, or someone else will edit the code. The folder structure here drops straight into a repository.

11. Maintenance

  • After the November lab results: change the nanotube and fullerene chips from "Specification to follow" to "Available for discussion now" (class chip ok), and add a specification line to each card.
  • When TIER recognition is confirmed: replace the last sentence of the TIER callout with the confirmed wording.
  • When social channels exist: add icon links in the footer and sameAs entries in the structured data.
  • When the re-set logo is delivered: replace the inline logo paths and regenerate the favicons and logo-512.png.
  • When GCM launches: point the buyers section to the GCM website and change its button to an outbound link.
