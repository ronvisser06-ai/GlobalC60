What we do

What Global C60 does

Global C60 Inc. is a Calgary company that removes CO2 from industrial emission streams and turns it into carbon products. We use carbon conversion technology licensed from Elemental Air.

Global C60 installs, operates and maintains the units, removes the carbon from site, and reports the results to you every month. Clients can host a unit at their site, or contract us to remove CO2 on a per-tonne basis.

Who we work with

  • Oil and gas operators
  • Gas-fired power producers, including those supplying data centres
  • Renewable natural gas (RNG) operators
  • Heavy industrial facilities
  • Any site that emits large volumes of CO2

What comes out

  • Less CO2 leaving your stack
  • A monthly, documented record of the CO2 removed
  • Solid carbon that is sold into industrial markets
  • Carbon credits created from the CO2 removed
How we work with you

Ways to work with us

Model 1

Host a unit

Global C60 places a unit at your site and runs it. You provide space, power and access to your flue gas stream.

Model 2

Carbon-as-a-Service (CaaS)

You contract Global C60 to remove CO2 from your emissions on a per-tonne basis. You pay for the tonnes removed, which lowers your CO2 emissions and your exposure to carbon taxes and compliance costs.

How a deployment works

  1. First conversation

    You tell us about your emission source, the volumes involved and the site.

  2. Site and stream review

    We look at your flue gas flow rate and CO2 concentration, available power, space and truck access.

  3. Agreement

    We agree the service model, site access and the monthly reporting you will receive.

  4. Installation

    We place the unit and a small, modular, temporary building on site, and connect to your flue gas stream and power.

  5. Operation and reporting

    We run the unit, truck the carbon off site, and send you a CO2 reduction report every month.

What your site provides

  • Space for the unit
  • Room for a small, modular, temporary building
  • Access to power
  • A connection to your flue gas stream
  • Access for trucks

What you receive

  • A monthly CO2 reduction report for the emission stream
  • A unit installed, operated and maintained by Global C60
  • CO2 removed from your stream, which is converted into solid carbon and creates carbon credits

Benefits

What it means for an emitter

Lower compliance cost

Carbon pricing in Canada, and in several US states and programs, puts a cost on each tonne of CO2 a facility emits. CO2 removed from your stream by a Global C60 unit is measured and documented, which can reduce what you owe or need to offset.

Documented reductions

The monthly report gives your environment and regulatory teams measured data for compliance filings and sustainability reporting.

Pay for results

Under a Carbon-as-a-Service (CaaS) contract you pay per tonne of CO2 removed. Global C60 installs, operates and maintains the unit. Your site provides space, power and access.

Waste becomes a product

Today the CO2 in your emissions is a waste stream. Global C60 turns that waste into solid carbon that is sold to industrial buyers, instead of being released to the air or stored underground.

Reduced at the source

CO2 is converted where it is produced. It is not captured and sent off site for sequestration, so your facility does not need a connection to CO2 pipeline and storage infrastructure. Modular units can significantly reduce the cost of carbon compliance.

Other emissions reduced

Along with CO2, a Global C60 unit reduces sulphur oxides (SOx), nitrogen oxides (NOx) and other carbon oxides such as carbon monoxide in the emission stream. It does not remove hydrogen sulphide (H2S).

Alberta facilities

Managing TIER obligations

Facilities regulated under Alberta's Technology Innovation and Emissions Reduction (TIER) Regulation must meet an emissions benchmark or cover the gap with fund payments, credits or offsets. A Global C60 unit removes CO2 at the source and gives you monthly measured data, which can help you manage your TIER obligations and plan for tightening benchmarks. Global C60 is in discussions with the TIER program on recognizing CO2 removed by our process for credit.

Under investigation

Methane-as-a-Service (MaaS)

Global C60 is investigating how to capture methane that is not combusted in engines and heaters, and methane redirected from flaring, and convert it into solid carbon. If you operate engines, heaters or flares and would like to be part of that work, register your interest.

Carbon products

The carbon we produce

Every Global C60 unit produces solid carbon. We are starting with a domestically produced carbon equivalent to carbon black. Lab-assessed specifications for our carbon nanotubes and fullerenes will be published once the current validation work is complete.

Carbon black equivalent

Available for discussion now

A fine black carbon powder, produced in North America from captured CO2, for use where carbon black is used today. Because it is a direct alternative to an existing material, it is expected to need less extensive qualification testing than a new specialty material before it can be added to, or replace, a current carbon black supply.

Industries that use carbon black
  • Tires and rubber products, including belts, hoses, seals and mouldings
  • Plastics and masterbatch, for colour, UV protection and antistatic grades
  • Printing inks and toners
  • Paints and coatings
  • Wire and cable jacketing
  • Conductive additives for batteries and electronics
  • Construction materials, as a pigment for concrete and roofing

Carbon nanotubes

Specification to follow

A layer of carbon atoms rolled into a seamless tube a few nanometres across and many times longer than it is wide. Nanotubes are very strong for their weight and conduct electricity well.

Single-wall nanotubes are one layer thick. They give the highest conductivity at very low loadings and are used in battery electrodes, conductive films and electronics.

Multi-wall nanotubes are several tubes nested inside each other. They are more robust and lower in cost, and are used as conductive additives in lithium-ion batteries, in polymer composites, antistatic plastics and coatings.

{{C60}}

Fullerenes (C60 and C70)

Specification to follow

Closed, hollow molecules made only of carbon. C60, the molecule our company is named after, has 60 carbon atoms arranged in 12 pentagons and 20 hexagons, the same pattern as a soccer ball. C70 is a slightly elongated version with 70 atoms.

Used in: organic solar cells and electronics, lubricant and polymer additives, cosmetics, and materials research.
